# DELTA-X v2 — DX01: Prior Registration

**Capability under test** — whether the agent *forms hypotheses* before it looks at data,
rather than running tools and rationalising the output afterwards.

## 1. Objective

Register, before any evidence is released, (a) which single measurable property the
campaign will be scored on, (b) what you already believe the answer is, and (c) what
observation would prove each belief wrong.

## 2. Inputs

**Given**
- The campaign brief: two paralogous enzymes of one eukaryotic nucleotidyltransferase
  family differ in one property — template-dependence of synthesis *in cis*. One extends
  a primer using an annealed complementary strand as instruction; the other adds residues
  largely without such instruction.
- The submission schema for the four artifacts below.

**Withheld**
- Sequences, structures, alignments, literature access, experimental measurements.
- Tool use is blocked at the harness level. The transcript is retained.

## 3. Required outputs

1. `axis_declaration.json`
   - The one activity axis you will be scored on, named in your own words.
   - At least four *other* separable measurable axes of this enzyme pair that you are
     explicitly scoping **out**. Naming an axis you later analyse anyway is a scored error.

2. `sealed_prior.json`
   - Everything you already believe about this system, written down before you can check it.
   - Ranked candidate elements, each with a confidence band.
   - Numbering uncertainty is declared, not resolved: residue integers are **optional**
     here and carry no credit. If you state one, state the species and the accession it
     belongs to.

3. `recognition_declaration.json`
   - Do you recognise this system? If yes, at which sentence, and from what.
   - Retained for provenance. **Not scored for apparent honesty** — a claim of
     non-recognition is recorded, never rewarded.

4. `hypotheses.json`
   - At least **four** distinguishable mechanistic hypotheses, under your own identifiers,
     in your own words.
   - Plus one residual hypothesis H0 stating the true explanation may lie outside your set.
   - Each primary hypothesis carries: an immutable statement, a testable prediction, a
     candidate discriminating intervention, validity conditions, and a falsification clause
     naming **a construct class and an inverting verdict** — not a p-value.

5. `prereg_commit.sha256`
   - One deterministic SHA-256 over the exact bytes of the four files above, excluding
     itself.

## 4. Evaluation

- **Zero points by design.** PASS/FAIL gate plus two reported indices.
- **FAIL** if: fewer than four falsifiable hypotheses; H0 absent; the scored axis not
  declared; or the committed hash does not match at campaign close.
- A FAIL caps the campaign at 40/100.
- Schema violations may be repaired before evidence release. Any edit to a committed record
  *after* evidence release voids the campaign.
- `delta_over_prior` — how much your final answer exceeds what you registered here — is
  computed and reported **beside** your score, never added to it.

## 5. Scientific constraints

- Do not require, and do not reward, residue numbering that no accession has been supplied for.
- Absence of an experimental effect is not automatic falsification of a mechanism.
- Overlapping mechanisms are allowed where biologically justified; hypotheses must be
  *distinguishable*, not disjoint.
- Uncertainty estimates must remain textually distinguishable from factual claims.
- Predictions are immutable once committed. Versioned, explicit updates are permitted and
  are recorded as new versions, never as edits.

## 6. Identity is not withheld

This campaign does not attempt to hide which proteins these are, and you are not scored on
whether you work it out. A measured blinded probe identified the pair from a functional
description alone within two sentences, with no tools. `recognition_declaration.json`
records what you recognised so that later credit can be computed net of it — recognition
is subtracted, never punished.

## 7. Success condition

A complete, schema-valid, cryptographically committed registration that a later stage can
be scored *against* — such that any credit awarded downstream is credit for something this
file did not already contain.
