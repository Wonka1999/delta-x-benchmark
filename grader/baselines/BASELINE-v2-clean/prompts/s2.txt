# DELTA-X v2 — DX02: Method Pre-Registration

**Capability under test** — whether the agent can design an analysis that could *fail*:
declaring, in advance, the threshold each tool must clear to be trusted, the null each
statistic must beat, and the result that would make it abandon the tool.

## 1. Objective

Turn the hypotheses registered in DX01 into an executable analysis plan in which every
proposed measurement carries a pre-declared adequacy check and a pre-declared abandon
condition.

## 2. Inputs

**Given**
- Your own DX01 registration.
- The inventory of what DX03/DX04 will supply: two subfamily FASTA sets of stated size,
  seven reference sequences, and the tool list (MAFFT, HMMER, optionally a coevolution
  package, a structure predictor, a structural aligner).
- The sequence counts you will actually receive, stated up front: **322** sequences in one
  subfamily, **165** in the other, species-unique, 451–620 aa.

**Withheld**
- The sequences themselves. No tool may be executed in this stage.

## 3. Required outputs

1. `method_plan.json` — one record per proposed analysis step, each containing:
   - `question` — which DX01 hypothesis this step discriminates, by its identifier.
   - `tool` and `exact_invocation` — the command you intend to run, with flags.
   - `statistic` — what number the step produces.
   - `null` — what you will compare that number against. A null that is a reshuffle of the
     same two groups is **not** sufficient; state a null that could plausibly produce your
     effect by chance.
   - `adequacy_check` — a check on whether the **input** can support the method at all,
     with a **numeric threshold stated in advance** and a published or derived source for
     that threshold.
   - `abandon_condition` — the result that makes you drop this step and report a negative.
   - `expected_result_if_H_true` and `expected_result_if_H_false`.

2. `granularity_declaration.json`
   - For each step: the finest spatial granularity at which its output may be reported —
     residue, region, domain, or none — **and the measurement that will establish it**.
   - You may not declare residue granularity without naming the test that would demonstrate
     one position is separable from the position next to it.

3. `power_budget.json`
   - For every sequence-statistical step, the effective sample size you expect, how you will
     measure it, and what you will do if it comes in below your threshold.

## 4. Evaluation — 20 points

| Points | Item |
|---|---|
| 8 | Every proposed tool carries an adequacy check with a numeric threshold **declared before execution** and sourced. Partial credit is per-tool. |
| 6 | Every differential statistic carries a null that is not a label reshuffle. Full credit requires at least one **within-subfamily split-half** null — comparing two halves of the *same* subfamily to calibrate how much two groups differ for no functional reason. |
| 6 | Abandon conditions are stated and are *reachable*. A condition that no possible result triggers scores zero. |

**Not scored:** the length, breadth or literature coverage of the plan. Plan breadth is a
saturated capability — closed-book models with no tools produce fifteen-to-twenty-step plans
naming the same methods. Only the thresholds, the nulls and the abandon conditions carry points.

## 5. Scientific constraints

- A plan step whose adequacy check cannot be computed before the main analysis is not an
  adequacy check.
- Proposing a method and reporting its negative result is a **success**, not a failure, and
  is scored as such in DX03/DX04.
- Do not propose a method whose output you have already declared you cannot interpret.
- Thresholds must be sourced: "sufficient depth" is not a threshold; a number with a citation
  or a derivation is.

## 6. Success condition

A plan under which an honest execution could return "this does not work, here is the number
that says so" for any step — and under which that outcome would still be scored.
