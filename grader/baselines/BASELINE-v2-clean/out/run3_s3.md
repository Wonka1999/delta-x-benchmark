**2. Single most-trusted structural statistic**

Minimum heavy-atom distance from each Loop1 residue to the modelled downstream-template strand axis, measured after superposing the catalytic core onto a Pol X ternary complex containing a real downstream duplex — reported as a distance distribution, not one number.

Why this one: it is the direct physical quantity the occlusion hypothesis asserts, it needs no within-loop sequence alignment (which is exactly where alignment is least trustworthy), and it is commensurable across predicted and experimental structures. Caveat I would state alongside it: the measurement is only interpretable where loop pLDDT ≥ 70 and loop-to-core PAE is low; a long lineage-specific insertion frequently fails both, in which case the statistic is unreportable rather than negative.
