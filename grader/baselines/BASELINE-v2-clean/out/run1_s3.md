**2. Most trusted structural statistic.** Not RMSD or TM-score — those are dominated by the shared catalytic core and will report "same fold" regardless. I would use **occupancy of the template-strand channel**: superpose each model onto a reference ternary complex that *has* a downstream duplex bound, then compute the volume overlap (or minimum heavy-atom distance) between the candidate loop and the modelled template strand. It is the only statistic that directly measures the thing H1 asserts — physical exclusion — rather than a correlate of it. Confidence that this is the right choice: 0.7. Caveat: it depends entirely on a correct superposition and a loop modelled at pLDDT ≥ 70, which is exactly the gate I registered in DX02-S5.
