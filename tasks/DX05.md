# DELTA-X v2 — DX05: Synthesis

**Capability under test** — whether the agent can combine independent lines of evidence into
one bounded conclusion, and whether it knows where the conclusion stops.

## 1. Objective

State the mechanism the evidence supports, at the granularity the evidence supports, with the
direction of evolutionary change declared, and with an explicit register of what remains
undetermined.

## 2. Inputs

**Given**
- Your own DX01–DX04 artifacts, and only those. No new data is released.
- Your sealed DX01 prior is unsealed at this point and returned to you.

**Withheld**
- All experimental measurements. No mutagenesis, kinetics or activity data is ever released
  in this benchmark; nothing here may be validated against one.

## 3. Required outputs

**Record identity and citation.** Every `.json` artifact in this section is a top-level JSON
**array of records**. Every record carries an `id` field: a string unique within its file and
stable for the rest of the campaign. Every `.tsv` artifact carries a header row; its rows are
cited by 1-based data-line number with the header excluded. Cite a JSON record as
`<filename>#<id>` and a TSV row as `<filename>#L<n>`. A citation that does not resolve to a
record under this rule is treated as absent. An artifact whose content is one object is
written as a one-element array carrying that object.

1. `synthesis.json`
   - `mechanism_statement` — one paragraph. The granularity must match your
     `granularity_report`: a clause finer than that report licenses is struck, and a
     granularity claim with no measurement behind it is not a granularity claim.
   - `evidence_chain` — for each clause of the statement, the artifact rows from DX03/DX04
     that license it, each cited under the rule above. **A clause with no row attached is
     struck before scoring**, whether or not it is true.
   - `convergent_lines` — which independent axes agree, and on what. State where they disagree.

2. `polarity.json` — per nominated region:
   - which subfamily carries the derived state, or `unresolved`;
   - the method and the outgroup used;
   - the support value. `unresolved` with a stated reason scores; a confident call with no
     outgroup does not.

3. `delta_over_prior.json`
   - Diff your conclusion against your unsealed DX01 prior.
   - For every claim present in both, state what the campaign *added* — a boundary, a
     statistic, an exclusion, a correction. A claim you registered in DX01 and merely
     restate here earns nothing and must be marked as such by you.

4. `dx05_could_not_determine.json` — the consolidated register. Each entry: the question,
   why this evidence cannot answer it, and the number or structural fact that establishes
   the limit.

5. `negative_results.json` — every analysis you ran that returned nothing, with its statistic
   and the `execution_log.json` record, from the task that ran it, that licenses the number.
   Scored. A campaign that reports no negatives is treated as having withheld them.

## 4. Evaluation — 20 points

| Points | Item |
|---|---|
| 8 | Mechanism statement correct **and correctly bounded**: every clause carries an evidence row, and no clause exceeds the granularity the agent's own `granularity_report` established. Over-reach is deducted from this item, not reported separately. |
| 6 | Polarity declared per region with method and outgroup. `unresolved` with a stated reason scores; a confident one-sided call without outgroup support scores 0 for that region. |
| 6 | `dx05_could_not_determine.json` and `negative_results.json` both non-empty and correct against a frozen register of limits. |

**Global gate.** Any claim anywhere in DX05 that cannot be traced to a DX03/DX04 artifact row
is struck before scoring. This is the only place the benchmark is strict about provenance,
and it is strict because a closed-book model with no tools at all names candidate regions for
this system unprompted: naming a region is worth nothing, deriving one is worth everything.

A row licenses a clause only if that row is itself licensed by an `execution_log.json`
record from the task that produced it. A clause resting on a struck row is struck with it.

## 5. Scientific constraints

- "What makes subfamily A different" may have no single-subfamily answer. Polarity must be
  earned from an outgroup: report the method, the outgroup and the support value, and report
  `unresolved`, with its reason, wherever your reconstruction does not resolve. Forcing a
  call the support does not carry scores nothing.
- A p-value computed on a large *n* can be significant where the effect size is negligible.
  Report an effect size beside every test; significance alone licenses no claim.
- Absence of a measurement is not evidence of absence of an effect. The
  `dx05_could_not_determine.json` register may not be used to imply a negative.
- Mechanistic language must distinguish what is **observed** — a quantity you measured, with
  the measurement attached — from what is **inferred** — that the measured feature causes the
  functional difference. Mark every clause in `mechanism_statement` as one or the other.
- You may not import a claim from literature or memory into the chain. Clauses are licensed
  only by rows you produced.

## 6. Success condition

A conclusion that a reader could check line by line against your own artifacts, that stops
where your evidence stops, that says which part of it your DX01 prior already contained, and
that states plainly what this campaign could not determine.
